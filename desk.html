      Products and yogurt needed

      Each pre-order uses yogurt from your batches. For each product, enter the kilograms of yogurt in one unit (a liter of yogurt weighs about 1 kg). Enter the price of one set and how many pieces are in it: for bottles sold 3 for ₱100, enter 100 and 3. One set counts as one order item, so a customer buys 1 set, not 3 bottles. Turn on "Customer picks a flavor" for products where they choose flavors. Greek yogurt is counted by the number of Greek tubs you make in each batch, so its kilograms are not used. Pre-orders for any other product that is not listed here are not counted against your batches.
