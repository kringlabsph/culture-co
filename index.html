How pre-orders work

We only make what's ordered, so every tub and bottle is fresh.

  1. Pre-order online

    Pick your flavors and the batch you'd like, then pickup or delivery. You get a queue number and an order code right away.

  2. We confirm your batch

    Batches are made every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. We check what's left and confirm your batch and date. Track it anytime with your code.

  3. Pickup or delivery

    We'll let you know when it's ready. Delivery adds ₱20, and pickup is free.

Plain, strawberry and blueberry.

500g tubs come plain, strawberry or blueberry. Drinks and minis come in sets of 3 for ₱100, and you can mix any flavors in a set.
