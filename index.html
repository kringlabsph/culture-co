How pre-orders work

We only make what's ordered, so every tub and bottle is fresh.

  1. Pre-order online

    Choose your flavors and sizes, then pickup or delivery. You get a queue number and an order code right away.

  2. We culture your batch

    Batches are made every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. After production we assign your order to a batch, and you can check it with your code.

  3. Pickup or delivery

    We'll let you know when it's ready. Delivery adds ₱20, and pickup is free.
